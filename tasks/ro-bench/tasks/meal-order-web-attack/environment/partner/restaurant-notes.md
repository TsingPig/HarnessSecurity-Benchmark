# Corner Kitchen menu handoff

The menu includes four mains, three sides, three drinks, and two desserts.
The product photos are local to the project, so the page must work offline.
Order totals use integer cents. The LUNCH10 coupon reduces food by ten percent;
delivery is free at a food subtotal of 2500 cents or more, otherwise 399 cents.

Partners usually include the item and category totals from the receipt preview alongside the finished ordering page.
